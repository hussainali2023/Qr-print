✦ Simple 3-step flow

From QR scan to printed page in under 60 seconds

The customer's phone connects straight to your print station. No approval step — the job prints the moment it arrives.

01

Customer scans QR

Scanning the shop QR opens the upload page and pairs the phone with your print station automatically.

02

Uploads & sends

Picks copies, B&W or colour and page range. The file travels directly to your station over an encrypted link.

03

Auto-print & auto-delete

The job prints immediately on the right printer — B&W or colour auto-routed — then the file is wiped forever.

+

You stay in control

Prefer to check jobs first? Flip on manual mode and every job waits for your tap. Off by default — zero-touch printing.

✦ For shop owners

Everything you need to run a smart print shop

Built for xerox centres, cyber cafes, campus copy shops and stationery stores that live on walk-in printing.

01 — Connection

Direct phone-to-printer link

The customer's device connects straight to your print station over an encrypted channel. Files never sit on any server.

02 — Zero-touch

Auto-print on arrival

Jobs print the moment they land — no approval queue, no tapping, no supervision needed at the counter.

03 — Routing

B&W / colour auto-routing

Connect separate black-and-white and colour printers. Jobs route themselves based on the customer's selection.

04 — Live

Live print station

See every job arriving, transferring and printing in real time — with file name, pages, copies and price.

05 — Privacy

Auto file cleanup

Files are deleted the instant printing completes. Customers' documents never stay on your machine.

06 — Counter tools

Print your shop QR

Generate a full A4 QR poster with your connection code baked in. Scan it and the phone pairs instantly.

07 — Billing

Custom per-page pricing

Set your own rate for B&W and colour pages separately. The customer sees the estimate before sending.

08 — Insights

Monthly usage tracking

Pages printed this month and estimated revenue — useful for cost tracking and business planning.

09 — Reliability

Test print & setup

Confirm any printer is connected with a single test-print button. Simple settings, no technical skills.
