✦ Simple 4-step flow

From QR scan to printed page in under 60 seconds

The whole workflow runs in the browser. The customer never installs anything, the shop owner never touches a pen drive.

01

Customer scans QR

Scanning the shop's QR opens the upload page instantly — no app download, no login, no account.

02

Uploads & selects

Picks copies, B&W or colour, and page range. The smart form estimates pages and the price live.

03

Owner approves

The order appears live on the shop dashboard with every detail. One tap to approve — or reject.

04

Auto-print & auto-delete

The job prints automatically to the right printer, then the file is permanently deleted. Zero data kept.

✦ For shop owners

Everything you need to run a smart print shop

Built for xerox centres, cyber cafes, campus copy shops and stationery stores that live on walk-in printing.

01 — Live queue

Live order dashboard

Every pending order appears in real time as customers submit them. No refresh, no WhatsApp file hunting.

02 — Order details

Full order details

File name, page count, copies, colour mode and order number — all visible at a glance on each order card.

03 — One click

Approve & auto-print

Approve an order and it prints automatically and silently to the correct printer — no further action.

04 — Routing

B&W / colour auto-routing

Connect separate black-and-white and colour printers. Orders route themselves based on customer selection.

05 — Privacy

Auto file cleanup

Files are deleted the moment printing completes. Customers' data never sits on your machine.

06 — Counter tools

Print your shop QR

Generate a full A4 QR poster for your counter or wall. Customers scan it to start printing immediately.

07 — Billing

Custom per-page pricing

Set your own rate for B&W and colour pages separately. Prices update instantly on the customer screen.

08 — Insights

Monthly usage tracking

See pages printed this month and estimated revenue — useful for cost tracking and business planning.

09 — Reliability

Test print & setup

Confirm any printer is connected with a single test-print button. Simple dropdown setup, no technical skills.
